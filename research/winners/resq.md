A single large SOS control ("Press and hold for 3 seconds") that fires location sharing to a trust network: one gesture, one outcome.

## Why this won (one sentence)
A women's-safety framing with one unmistakable interaction was enough for 3rd place and the beginner award at a girls-in-STEM event, even with scope that was largely designed rather than built.

## Transferable to us
- **Copy:** one dominant control, a design principle stated in the README ("3 taps or less"), accessibility named explicitly (high contrast, haptics, voice).
- **Don't copy:** presenting wireframes as working features; that is the gap judges find. Everything we show must run.
